Capabilities

HST/MAST Pipeline

Cache-first FITS retrieval from MAST for 9 SLACS lens systems with PSF-convolved forward modeling and image-space diagnostics.

PINN Inference

JAX/Equinox physics-informed neural network with Poisson constraint ∇²ψ = 2κ (Schneider 1992) for mass reconstruction from convergence maps.

Wave Optics

Nakamura & Deguchi (1999) scalar diffraction integral F(ω) for gravitational wave lensing magnification with frequency-dependent signatures.

Nested Sampling

Pure-NumPy Skilling (2004) evidence computation with Jeffreys-scale Bayesian model comparison between NFW and SIS profiles.

SLACS Validation

5-system observational validation pipeline: PSF-convolved lensed Sérsic fitting with NRMSE, SSIM, and annular ring correlation thresholds.

PI-SBI Amortized

First joint EM+GW amortized posterior estimator with physics-constrained encoder and 8-layer RealNVP flow — ~10,000× over MCMC.
